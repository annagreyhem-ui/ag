The book

How life feels… is not a novel. It is an adventure through the depths of a soul — read forwards, understood backwards. Readers might feel everything at once.

The form

The Book lives in four movements — Becoming, Longing, Falling, Shifting. It's autofiction that won't behave. Poems bleed into prose, stories fracture, and some pages reach past the book into immersive digital experiences.

How life feels... ist the first Book by Anna Greyhem — written somewhere between a breakdown and a breakthrough. There is many more to come.

I write books you don't just read. You enter them.

Scan a code hidden in the margins. Find a password buried in a chapter. Make a choice that takes you somewhere the printed page never could. I build worlds that exist between ink and screen, between what you hold in your hands and what opens when you look closer.

How Life Feels is my first book. It won't be my last. I spent years doing other things — sensible things — before I finally allowed myself to be what I always was.

A writer. An artist. Someone who can't tell a story without wanting you to live inside it.

The book you'll find here is not just literature. It's an experience. Part novel, part game, part digital world. Anna and Alice are waiting. The house has rooms you haven't found yet. Some doors only open at night.

I am the space between them. Between the one who loves life so fiercely it burns her, and the one who has already made peace with the dark.

I didn't want to write a book you finish. I wanted to write one you can't leave.
